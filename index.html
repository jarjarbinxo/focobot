Is 29 or 59 KWD really worth it for a small business? +
Consider this: if Focobot helps you convert even one extra customer per week — at an average order value of 20 KWD — that's 80 KWD/month in new revenue just from the bot working while you sleep. Most of our Starter plan customers see positive ROI within their first 7 days. The 7-day free trial lets you prove it before paying a single fils.
Does it actually speak proper Kuwaiti Arabic, or is it awkward translation? +
Focobot is specifically trained on Gulf Arabic and Kuwaiti dialect — not Google Translate. We use natural, conversational language that your customers feel comfortable with. You can also review and customise every response before launch so it matches your brand's voice exactly. Many of our customers say their clients assume they're talking to a human staff member.
How long does setup actually take? I'm not technical. +
You don't need to be technical at all. You fill out a simple onboarding form (about 5–10 minutes) describing your business, products, and common customer questions. Our team handles all the technical setup and has your bot live within 24 hours. We also provide a full walkthrough call so you're confident before we go live.
Am I locked into a contract? What if I want to cancel? +
Absolutely no contracts. Focobot is month-to-month. Cancel anytime from your dashboard with one click and you won't be charged again. We're confident enough in our product that we don't need to trap you — we'd rather earn your business every month. Your WhatsApp number always remains yours.
What happens when the bot can't answer a question? +
Focobot knows when to hand off. If a customer asks something outside its knowledge or explicitly asks for a human, it immediately alerts you and transfers the conversation — with full context — to your phone. You step in exactly where you're needed. Over time, we continuously train the bot on new questions so it handles more and more without your help.
Is this compliant with WhatsApp's Business Terms of Service? +
Yes. Focobot uses the official WhatsApp Business API, which is the only approved method for business automation. Your account will not be banned or flagged. We hold a verified WhatsApp Business Solution Provider partnership. Unlike grey-market tools, we build on Meta's approved infrastructure — your business and number are fully protected.
