Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Still unsure? These are the questions we hear most from Kuwait businesses before they sign up.

Is 29 KWD per month really worth it? +
If your bot captures just one extra booking or sale per week that you'd have otherwise missed, it's already paid for itself. Most Starter customers recover the subscription cost within the first 3 days. The Growth plan at 59 KWD is the equivalent of a part-time employee — except it works 24/7 and never makes a mistake.
Does it really understand Arabic properly? +
Yes — genuine, natural Arabic including Gulf dialect. Not machine-translated English. We train each bot specifically on your business vocabulary and tone in both languages. It detects which language the customer is writing in and responds accordingly, mid-conversation if needed.
How long does setup take? +
Most businesses are live within 24–48 hours. You fill in a 15-minute questionnaire about your services and tone. We build, train, and test the bot. You review and approve it. Then we connect it to your WhatsApp Business number and you're live. No technical knowledge needed from your side.
Am I locked into a contract? +
No contracts, no lock-in. Pay month to month. Cancel anytime with no penalty. We're confident you'll stay because of results — not because of paperwork.
What happens when a customer asks something the bot doesn't know? +
On Growth and Enterprise plans, the bot gracefully hands off to your team with a full transcript so your staff can pick up seamlessly. On Starter, it sends a polite message saying a team member will be in touch, and flags the conversation for you. No customer is ever left hanging with an error message.
Do I need a special WhatsApp number or account? +
You need a WhatsApp Business account — which is free and takes about 10 minutes to set up if you don't have one. Focobot connects to your existing business number, so your customers message the same number they already know. We guide you through the connection process step by step.
