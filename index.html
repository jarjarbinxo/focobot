FAQ

Questions we
actually get asked

Does it really reply in 2 seconds? +
Yes — Focobot’s AI processes and replies in under 2 seconds, around the clock. No queuing, no delays, no “we’ll get back to you.” Your customer gets a real, helpful answer instantly.
Do I need to change my WhatsApp number? +
No. Focobot works with your existing WhatsApp Business number. Customers keep messaging the same number they already know — you just add the intelligence layer on top.
Does it really understand Kuwaiti Arabic? +
Yes — trained specifically on Gulf dialect, Kuwaiti expressions, and the informal mix of Arabic and English that real customers use. It auto-detects the language and responds in kind, no configuration needed.
How long does setup take? +
Most businesses are live within 10 minutes. You connect your WhatsApp, paste in your business info, and switch it on. Our team is on WhatsApp to help if anything takes longer.
What if a customer has a complex question? +
Focobot handles 90%+ of questions automatically. For anything complex or sensitive, it flags the conversation and notifies you — so you only step in when it truly matters, not for every “what time do you close?”
Is there a contract or setup fee? +
Zero setup fees and no contracts. Every plan is month-to-month. Cancel anytime from your dashboard or message us on WhatsApp. We prefer you stay because you love it, not because you’re locked in.
Is my customer data safe? +
All data is encrypted end-to-end and hosted locally in Kuwait. We never sell, share, or use your customer data for anything outside your account. Full stop.
Can I try it before I pay? +
Yes — 14 days free, no credit card required. You get full Growth plan access during the trial so you can see real results before you decide.
