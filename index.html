FAQ

Questions we
actually get asked

Does it really reply in 2 seconds? +
Yes — Focobot's AI processes and replies in under 2 seconds, around the clock. No queuing, no delays, no "we'll get back to you." Your customer gets a real, helpful answer instantly.
Do I need to change my WhatsApp number? +
No. Focobot works with your existing WhatsApp Business number. Customers keep messaging the same number they already know. You just add the intelligence layer on top.
What languages does it support? +
Focobot handles Arabic, English, and Kuwaiti dialect fluently. It auto-detects what language the customer is writing in and responds in the same language — no configuration needed.
What if a customer has a complex question? +
Focobot handles 90%+ of questions automatically. For anything complex or sensitive, it flags the conversation and notifies you, so you can step in when it truly matters — not for every "what time do you close?"
Is my customer data safe? +
Absolutely. All data is encrypted end-to-end and hosted locally in Kuwait. We never sell, share, or use your customer data for anything outside your account. Full stop.
How long does setup take? +
Most businesses are live within 10 minutes. You connect your WhatsApp, paste in your business info, and switch it on. Our team is on WhatsApp to help if anything takes longer.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, no contracts and no cancellation fees. Cancel anytime from your dashboard or just message us on WhatsApp. We prefer you stay because you love it, not because you're locked in.
