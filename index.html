✦ FAQ

Questions Answered,
Objections Cleared

Everything you need to know before you start your free trial.

Is 59 KWD a month really worth it for a small business? +
One recovered customer per month is typically 20–200 KWD in revenue. Focobot recovers dozens of missed conversations every night. Our Growth customers average a 3.8× return on their subscription in the first 60 days — most cover the full annual cost from a single weekend of automated replies. The real question is: how much is each unanswered WhatsApp costing you?
Does Focobot actually understand Arabic and Gulf dialect? +
Yes — this is one of our core strengths. Focobot is trained on Gulf Arabic including Kuwaiti dialect, colloquial expressions, code-switching (mixing Arabic and English mid-sentence), and common WhatsApp shorthand. It replies naturally in whatever language the customer uses. No stiff formal Arabic, no robotic English — it sounds like a well-trained member of your team.
How long does setup actually take? +
Most businesses are live within 24 hours. Here's the breakdown: connecting your WhatsApp number takes about 5 minutes, uploading your business information (menu, services, FAQs, prices) takes 1–2 hours, and the first round of testing takes another 30 minutes. Our onboarding team guides you through every step via a dedicated WhatsApp support line — included free on every plan.
Am I locked into a long contract? +
No contracts, no lock-in. All plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime with zero fees. We also offer annual billing at a 15% discount if you choose to commit — but there's never any pressure to do so. We're confident that once you see your first week of automated replies, you won't want to go back.
What if Focobot gives a wrong answer to a customer? +
Focobot only answers questions based on the information you provide — it doesn't guess or make things up. When it encounters a question outside its knowledge base, it either escalates to a human team member with a notification, or sends a polite "let me connect you with our team" message. You're always in control of which topics Focobot handles and which it escalates.
Do I need to install any software or have technical skills? +
Zero technical skills needed. Everything runs through our web dashboard — no apps to install, no coding required. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Focobot. We've designed specifically for busy business owners in Kuwait who don't have time to learn complicated software.
