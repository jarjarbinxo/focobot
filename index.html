✦ FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers

Is KWD 59/month worth it for my small business?
+
Think of it this way: the average order in Kuwait is 8–15 KWD. If Focobot captures just 5–8 extra sales per month from messages you'd have missed, it's paid for itself many times over. Our customers report an average 40% increase in WhatsApp-sourced revenue within 30 days.
Does Focobot really understand Arabic — including Gulf dialect?
+
Yes. Focobot is trained on Kuwaiti and Gulf Arabic, not just Modern Standard Arabic. It understands colloquial phrases, handles mixed Arabic-English messages (like "ابي order delivery please"), and responds naturally in whichever language the customer prefers.
How long does setup take? Do I need technical skills?
+
Most businesses are live in 30 minutes. No technical skills needed — it's a simple form where you describe your business, upload your menu or service list, set your hours, and add FAQs. Our onboarding team is on WhatsApp to guide you through every step.
Am I locked into a contract?
+
No contracts, ever. Focobot is month-to-month. Cancel, upgrade, or downgrade from your dashboard at any time. We earn your business every month by delivering results — not by trapping you in commitments.
What if Focobot can't answer a question?
+
When Focobot isn't confident, it politely tells the customer it's connecting them with a human and sends you an immediate notification. You take over seamlessly. You can also set custom escalation rules — e.g., always transfer complaints or large orders to a human agent.
Is my customer data safe and stored in Kuwait?
+
Your data never leaves the Gulf region. Focobot uses encrypted, compliant infrastructure hosted in the Gulf, fully aligned with Kuwait data protection requirements. Customer conversations are never shared with third parties or used to train external AI models.
