Common Questions

Everything You
Want to Know

Is 29 KWD worth it for a small business?
+
If you receive even 5 WhatsApp inquiries per day and convert one more per week, Focobot pays for itself. The average Kuwaiti small business recovers their monthly subscription within 3 days of going live — just from orders they would have missed overnight. The question isn't whether it's worth it. It's how much you're currently losing without it.
Does Focobot actually handle Arabic properly — not just translate?
+
Yes. Focobot is trained on Gulf Arabic, Kuwaiti dialect, and Modern Standard Arabic. It understands shorthand (شكراً = شكرا), code-switching (mixing English and Arabic mid-sentence), and even Arabized brand names. It doesn't translate English responses — it thinks in Arabic natively.
How long does it take to set up?
+
Most businesses are live the same day they sign up. Connecting your WhatsApp number takes 15 minutes. Training Focobot on your products, prices, and FAQs takes 2–3 hours (we guide you through it). You don't need a developer or any technical knowledge. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can run Focobot.
Am I locked into a contract?
+
No contracts, no lock-in. Focobot is month-to-month on all plans. Cancel anytime with one message to our support team. We also offer a 14-day money-back guarantee — if you're not happy in the first two weeks for any reason, you get a full refund. No questions, no paperwork.
Will customers know they're talking to a bot?
+
Focobot can be configured to identify itself as an automated assistant (which many customers appreciate for speed) or to respond under your business name. Either way, the response quality is high enough that most customers don't mind — they care about getting a fast, accurate answer, not whether it came from a human.
What happens when the bot can't answer something?
+
Focobot escalates gracefully. When it encounters a question outside its training, it either offers to connect the customer with your team, asks them to call during business hours, or collects their details for a callback — depending on how you configure it. No customer is left hanging with an error message.
