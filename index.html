Questions

Everything You Want to
Ask Before You Sign Up

Is 29 KWD really worth it? What's the ROI?
If Focobot converts just one additional customer per day who would have left after an unanswered message, and your average transaction is 10 KWD, that's 300 KWD/month — a 10× return on the Starter plan. Most clients see their first converted booking within 24 hours of going live.
Will my Kuwaiti customers know they're talking to a bot?
Focobot is designed to feel natural. It uses Gulf Arabic, matches your brand tone, and handles mixed-language messages gracefully. When a question is too complex or personal, it escalates seamlessly to your team. You control the handoff rules. Most businesses find their customers prefer the instant response over waiting hours for a human reply.
How long does setup take? I'm not technical.
Average setup is 48 hours, and we do most of the work. You connect your WhatsApp Business number, share your menu or FAQ document, and answer a quick questionnaire about your business. Our Kuwait-based team handles the rest. No developers needed. We're available on WhatsApp (naturally) the whole time.
Am I locked into a contract?
No contracts, ever. All plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime from your dashboard with one click. We've never had a client cancel after the first month — but we want you to feel confident, not trapped. Your data is yours and we'll export it for you if you ever leave.
Does Focobot support Arabic only, or English too?
Both, fluently. Focobot detects the customer's language automatically and responds in kind — even if they switch mid-conversation. It handles Gulf Arabic dialect, formal Arabic, English, and mixed messages. You can also configure a default language and fallback behavior for specific conversation types.
Is my WhatsApp account safe with Focobot?
Yes. Focobot connects through the official Meta WhatsApp Business API — the same route used by major banks and airlines. Your account is never at risk of being banned (unlike unofficial automation tools). All data is encrypted at rest and in transit, stored on EU-compliant servers, and never sold to third parties.
