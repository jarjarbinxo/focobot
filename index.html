FAQ

Honest Answers to
Real Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How much does it actually cost? Are there hidden fees?+
Focobot starts at 29 KWD/month with no setup fees, no onboarding fees, and no hidden charges. The price you see is what you pay. VAT is included in all plans. You can cancel anytime — we don't lock you into contracts.
Does Focobot support Arabic? What about Kuwaiti dialect?+
Yes — fully. Focobot is built from the ground up for Arabic and English. It detects the customer's language automatically and replies naturally, including Gulf dialect Arabic. Your Kuwaiti customers will feel like they're talking to a local.
How long does setup take? Do I need technical skills?+
Most businesses go live in under 15 minutes. No technical skills required whatsoever. You fill out a form about your business, review the AI replies we generate, connect your WhatsApp Business number, and you're live. Our team is on hand to help via WhatsApp if you need it.
Can I still talk to customers manually? Does Focobot take over everything?+
You're always in control. Focobot handles repetitive questions (pricing, availability, hours, etc.) and you can jump into any conversation at any time. You can also set rules: if a customer asks to speak to a human, Focobot hands it over immediately with full conversation context.
Is my data and my customers' data safe?+
Absolutely. Focobot is built with enterprise-grade encryption and full WhatsApp Business API compliance. Your customer data stays in Kuwait-region servers and is never shared, sold, or used to train other systems. We are fully compliant with Kuwaiti data privacy standards.
What if I want to cancel? Is there a long-term commitment?+
No commitment, no contracts. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your current billing period. We're confident you'll stay because you'll see real results — but we never hold you hostage.
