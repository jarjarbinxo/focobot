✦ FAQ

Your Questions, Answered

Still on the fence? These are the questions we hear most from Kuwait business owners before they sign up.

Is 29 KWD/month actually worth it for a small business? +
Think of it this way: if Focobot captures just one additional sale per month that you would have missed — one customer who texted at midnight and got an instant reply instead of silence — it's already paid for itself. Most of our Starter plan customers see 15–30 new captured leads in their first month alone. The question isn't whether 29 KWD is worth it. It's how much revenue you're currently losing to unanswered messages.
Does Focobot actually support Arabic properly? +
Yes — and not just passable Arabic. Focobot detects whether a customer is writing in Arabic, English, or a mix of both, and responds in the same language naturally. We work with local linguists to ensure replies feel Kuwaiti, not translated. All Arabic text is displayed right-to-left correctly in WhatsApp. Dialect-aware responses are part of every plan.
How long does setup actually take? +
Connecting your WhatsApp takes 3 minutes — just scan a QR code. From there, our team builds your custom conversation flows based on a brief onboarding call. Most businesses are fully live within 24 hours. Growth plan customers get a dedicated onboarding specialist who handles everything and trains your team.
Am I locked into a contract? +
Absolutely not. Focobot is month-to-month, no annual commitment required. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time from your dashboard with one click. We believe in earning your business every month, not locking you in. That said, businesses that try Focobot for 30 days typically stay — the results make it an obvious decision.
Can I use my existing WhatsApp Business number? +
Yes. You keep your existing number and all your chat history. Customers keep messaging you the same way they always have — they won't notice any change except that you now reply instantly every time. We support both regular WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business accounts.
What if I get more messages than my plan allows? +
We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit so you're never caught off guard. You can upgrade to the Growth plan (unlimited messages) at any point mid-month and we'll prorate the difference. We never cut off your service or leave messages unanswered — your customers will always get a reply.
