Yes. Focobot is trained on Gulf-dialect Arabic and switches naturally between Arabic and English mid-conversation. Your customers genuinely can't tell it's AI — we've tested with hundreds of real Kuwaiti shoppers. The bot even understands colloquial terms and responds in kind.
