+
Yes. Focobot is trained specifically on Gulf Arabic including Kuwaiti dialect, slang, and the Arabic-English code-switching that most Kuwaiti customers actually use when messaging a business. It handles voice-to-text Arabic messages too. Send your trickiest customer messages during your trial and see for yourself.

Most businesses are live within 48 hours. No technical skills required. You connect WhatsApp with a QR code scan, share your business info (services, prices, FAQs), and our team builds your automation flows for you. All Growth and Enterprise plans include a dedicated onboarding call where we set up, test, and go live together.
Am I locked into a long contract?
+
No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel any time from your dashboard in 2 clicks. If you cancel, your full data is exported to you. That said, 94% of businesses that try Focobot for 30 days keep it — because it pays for itself consistently.
What happens when a customer asks something Focobot can't answer?
+
Focobot automatically flags complex questions and hands them off to you with full conversation context — so you're never starting from scratch. You get notified only for conversations that genuinely need a human. You can set business hours for handoffs so after-hours messages are held and delivered with a summary each morning.

All data is encrypted and stored on servers compliant with Gulf data protection standards. We never sell or share customer data. Focobot uses the official WhatsApp Business API — the same infrastructure used by major banks and airlines. Your data belongs to you, always.
