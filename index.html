FAQ

Questions We Hear
from Kuwait Businesses

Is 29–59 KWD/month worth it for a small business?
Consider what one missed sale costs you. If a customer messages at midnight, gets no reply, and orders from a competitor — that's lost revenue. Most of our Kuwait clients recover the cost of Focobot in the first week from sales they would have otherwise missed. The Starter plan at 29 KWD/month is less than one dinner for two in Kuwait City.
Does it really understand Kuwaiti Arabic?
Yes — Focobot is trained specifically on Gulf dialect Arabic, including Kuwaiti slang, colloquialisms, and code-switching (mixing Arabic and English in the same sentence, which is very common in Kuwait). Customers consistently tell our clients they didn't realize they were talking to a bot. We also support formal Modern Standard Arabic for businesses that prefer it.
How long does setup actually take?
Most businesses are live in 24 hours or less. You share your business information (menu, prices, FAQ, working hours) — we handle the rest. The WhatsApp Business API connection takes about 10 minutes on your end, then our team configures and tests your bot. No code, no technical knowledge required. We've onboarded 218 businesses and the longest setup was 3 days for a complex multi-branch operation.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment?
No contracts, no lock-in. Pay month to month and cancel anytime from your dashboard. We're confident enough in our results that we don't need to trap you. Start with a 7-day free trial — no credit card required — and only upgrade when you've seen the value yourself.
Will my WhatsApp account get banned?
Absolutely not. Focobot uses the official WhatsApp Business API, approved by Meta. This is the same infrastructure used by airlines, banks, and major retailers worldwide. Grey-market tools that clone WhatsApp or use unofficial methods are what get accounts banned — we're the safe, official alternative. Your account and your customer data are always protected.
What happens when a customer has a question the bot can't answer?
Focobot knows its limits. When a customer asks something outside its training, it smoothly hands the conversation over to your human team with a full context summary — so your staff never has to ask "what did you need?" again. You can also set custom escalation triggers (e.g., "send to human if customer says 'complaint' or 'manager'").
