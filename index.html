How much does it really cost — any hidden fees?
+
The price you see is everything. No setup fees, no per-message charges beyond your plan, no contract minimums. Starter at 29 KWD/mo and Growth at 59 KWD/mo, billed monthly. Cancel any time from your dashboard — no calls, no hassle.
Does it actually understand Kuwaiti Arabic dialect?
+
Yes — this was our #1 priority. Focobot handles Gulf Arabic, Kuwaiti dialect, Modern Standard Arabic, and English. It also auto-detects which language each customer prefers and responds in kind. If a customer writes in Arabizi (Arabic written in Latin letters), it handles that too.
How long does setup actually take?
+
Most businesses are live within 15 minutes. You connect your WhatsApp Business number, answer 10 questions about your business, and optionally upload a product catalog. Our AI trains automatically. You don't need a developer, any technical knowledge, or any special tools beyond your phone.
Will my customers know they're talking to a bot?
+
Only if you want them to. You can configure Focobot to be transparent ("Hi, I'm Focobot, your automated assistant") or to operate under your brand ("Hi, I'm Layla from Al-Anzi Electronics"). The AI responses are conversational and natural — many of our clients' customers never realize. You also control when to hand conversations to a human agent.
Is there a long-term contract or lock-in?
+
No contracts, no lock-in. Month-to-month billing. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time from your dashboard. If you cancel, you keep access until your billing period ends. We believe the results speak for themselves — we don't need contracts to keep you.
What if a customer asks something the bot can't answer?
+
Focobot gracefully escalates to a human when needed. You set the rules: which topics trigger escalation, which hours to escalate, and which team members to alert. The customer never hits a dead end. You'll get a WhatsApp notification the moment a conversation needs your attention.
Is my customers' data safe and private?
+
Absolutely. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share your customer data with third parties. Focobot is fully compliant with Meta's WhatsApp Business API terms. You own your data and can export or delete it any time.
