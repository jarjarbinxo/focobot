FAQ

Questions Kuwaiti
Businesses Always Ask

Is 29 KWD a month worth it for a small business?
One missed sale on a typical slow day often costs more than the entire monthly plan. Focobot typically pays for itself within the first 48 hours of being live. Our Starter customers average 8x ROI in their first month — every sale after that is pure gain.
Does it really support Arabic and the Gulf dialect?
Yes — natively. Focobot was trained on Kuwaiti and Gulf Arabic conversations, not just MSA. It handles mixed Arabic-English messages, code-switching, and slang that other bots get confused by. We review the bot's Arabic with native speakers before every deployment.
How long does setup take?
Most businesses go live within 24 hours of signing up. Our onboarding team connects your WhatsApp number, learns your products and FAQs, and runs live tests before handing it over. No code. No technical knowledge required — just a 20-minute onboarding call with us.
Are there long-term contracts or lock-in?
None. Pay month to month, cancel anytime from your dashboard. We keep customers by delivering results, not by trapping them in contracts. Less than 2% of our customers have ever cancelled — because cancelling would mean giving up revenue they now depend on.
What if the bot doesn't know the answer?
Focobot detects when it is out of its depth and transfers the conversation to you — along with the full chat history, so you step in with complete context. You also get an instant WhatsApp notification so you never miss the handoff.
Is my customers' data safe and compliant?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We operate on GCC-region servers and never sell or share your customer data. Our platform is designed to align with Kuwaiti telecommunications and data protection guidelines. Your data belongs to you — always.
