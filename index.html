Questions

Honest Answers to Real Questions

Plans start at 29 KWD/month (Starter) and 59 KWD/month (Growth). Enterprise is custom. Every new account gets a 7-day free trial with no credit card required — you can use the full Growth plan and only pay if you love it. We also offer a 7-day money-back guarantee after you subscribe.
Focobot is built specifically for Gulf Arabic — not a generic translation. It understands Kuwaiti dialect, switches between Arabic and English mid-conversation, and writes in the same tone your brand uses. Most customers can't tell they're talking to AI. You can customize the personality, formality level, and vocabulary to match your brand.
Most businesses are live in 15–20 minutes. There's no coding required. You connect your WhatsApp Business number, paste your product info and FAQ, and Focobot learns your business automatically. Our onboarding team also schedules a free 30-minute call to help you get set up perfectly — available in Arabic or English.
No contracts, no lock-in. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime from your dashboard with one click. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your current billing period. We don't hold your data hostage — you can export everything.
Focobot knows its limits. If a question is outside its training, it politely tells the customer it's connecting them with a team member — and it immediately alerts you via WhatsApp or email. You can jump in and take over the conversation seamlessly. You can also set specific triggers: "if customer asks about X, always escalate to human."
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256. Servers are hosted in EU data centers with full GDPR compliance. We never sell your customer data, never use it to train AI models for other customers, and we sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with all Enterprise clients. Your data is 100% yours.
